Rose Quartz Salon Privacy Policy

Rose Quartz Salon (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we may collect, use, and share information when you visit our salon, book or receive services, contact us, or interact with our website or online pages.

Information We May Collect We may collect information you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, appointment details, service preferences, and communications with us. If you make a payment, payment information is handled by our payment processors; we may receive limited transaction details. We may also collect information about how you use our website or online pages, including through cookies or similar technologies where applicable.

How We Use Information We may use information to schedule and provide appointments and services, communicate with you, process payments, respond to requests, manage our business, improve our services, and send promotional messages where permitted by law. You can opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link or contact us to opt out of other promotional messages.

How We Share Information We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as appointment-booking, payment-processing, communications, and website providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal information for money. We may use cookies or similar technologies, and some third parties may process information on our behalf.

Your Choices and Privacy Rights Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, contact Rose Quartz Salon through the contact method you normally use to reach us. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can also adjust your browser settings to manage cookies, though some website features may not work as intended.

Data Retention and Security We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children’s Privacy Our services and online pages are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated effective date, where applicable. Your continued interaction with us after an update means you have had the opportunity to review the revised policy.

Contact Us For questions or requests regarding this policy or your personal information, contact Rose Quartz Salon through the contact method you normally use to reach the salon.
